Slooop is an unofficial continuation of the open-source Dashchan imageboard browser for Android 11 and newer.

Features include:

<ul>
<li>Built-in support for Dvach, including reading and posting</li>
<li>Experimental read-only 4chan support</li>
<li>A native FFmpeg-based player for WebM, MP4, MOV, H.264, HEVC, VP8, VP9 and AV1 media</li>
<li>Image and video gallery, reverse image search and background media saving</li>
<li>Local thread archives with an integrated viewer</li>
<li>Favorites, reply notifications, filters and automatic bumping</li>
<li>Built-in, downloadable and user-provided themes and fonts</li>
<li>Accessibility options including scalable text and volume-key font controls</li>
</ul>

The F-Droid build does not contain the application self-updater or the optional Google Play Services security-provider integration. Imageboard extensions are distributed as separate APK files. Slooop always explains this and asks for explicit confirmation before downloading an extension in the F-Droid build.

Imageboards contain user-generated material which may include adult or otherwise offensive content.
