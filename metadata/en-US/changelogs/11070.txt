Added an optional Google ML Kit offline translator. The approximately 50 MB add-on is installed separately and does not increase the size of Slooop. The language pack is downloaded separately as well.
Added Apachan support for browsing boards and threads, loading images, previewing replies, and posting messages. Handling of current forum links has also been updated.
Added a Show board toggle to My Boards settings, allowing source board labels to be hidden separately for each feed.
Moved optional fonts to the add-ons catalog, where they can be downloaded as needed. A font that is already selected remains active after updating.
Added pinch-to-zoom for videos up to 10×. When double-tap seeking is disabled, double-tapping pauses or resumes playback. This behavior will be refined further in the next release.
Partially reduced delays and stuttering in the navigation drawer on some devices. Opening large threads and switching to history are now faster, and duplicate drawer pages have been fixed.
Moved the photo editor and video volume boost to Media, and My Boards to Forums.
