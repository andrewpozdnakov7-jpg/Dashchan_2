**Slooop 3.2.82**

- Fixed cursor and text selection colors to match the theme.
- Improved predictive Back in the gallery for videos, animations, loading attachments and after paging between files.
- A compatible grid preview is retained when the app is normally moved to the background.
- Fixed the compressed post background next to the floating thread menu.
- Added the experimental Update video previews setting: after returning, the thumbnail is replaced with the last viewed video frame. Disabled by default.
- Expanded diagnostics for reasons why a predictive preview is unavailable.
