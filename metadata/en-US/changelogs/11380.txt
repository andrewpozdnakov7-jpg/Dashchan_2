Introduced an experimental Material 3-inspired interface with a redesigned side menu and animations for screen, thread, gallery and reply form transitions. Enable it in Settings → Experimental features → New interface animations.
The new interface includes compact video controls, a central playback button and seeking with position updates while dragging the slider.
Settings search now includes search history and typo-tolerant matching.
Settings have been moved to appropriate sections. Offline translation has its own page, and favorite thread monitoring is now under Replies and notifications.
Tapping the empty space around an image, video or GIF no longer closes the viewer by default. The previous behavior can be enabled in media settings.
Photo editor blur, mosaic and cover regions can be moved, scaled and rotated. Text and stickers can be placed below effects or above them.
