**Slooop 3.2.73**

- Continuing to improve experimental Predictive Back on Android 14+ 😺 Added photo-to-thumbnail return in the gallery grid, a preview of the previous post in internal reply history, and previews of previous pages in the private browser and Reddit reader.
- Fixed the extra Back gesture needed in settings search after hiding the keyboard. Improved gesture cancellation, repeated Back and focus-loss handling in the gallery.
- Updated photo opening and upward-swipe closing animations. Loading the original image no longer interrupts a transition that started from its thumbnail.
- Refined the Material 3 interface: predictive Back warnings, autohide, download dialogs, dropdown lists and explanatory text in settings.
- Fixed repeated offers to install an app version that is already installed.

Predictive Back remains experimental: previews are not yet available for every transition.

Security features are still in testing.

Thanks to everyone for testing, logs and help finding bugs! 🐾
