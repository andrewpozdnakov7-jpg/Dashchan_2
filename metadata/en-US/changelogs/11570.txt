**Slooop 3.2.77**

- Reworked My posts and replies settings to separate history, reply checks and notifications more clearly. Favorite-thread checking settings have moved to Favorites.
- Added a separate settings text size option from 30 to 500%, with a preview.
- Redesigned Statistics: responsive cards replace the wide table. Fixed the horizontal scrolling issue.
- Updated swipe-to-hide threads in Material 3 with a background, icon, Hide label and smooth animations.
- Unified checkboxes and radio buttons throughout the app, including the legacy interface. Improved their visibility across themes.
- Removed abrupt flashing of the Done button and images when selecting captcha symbols.
- Fixed a crash when sharing images and videos to Slooop from other apps.
- Refined slider and loading indicator colors, discussion status styling and Favorites selection.

Thanks to everyone for testing, logs and help finding bugs! 🐾
