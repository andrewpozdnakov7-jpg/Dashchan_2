**Slooop 3.2.80**

- History text size now follows the side menu text scale.
- When replying with a swipe, the reply form starts opening while the post card returns to its original position.
- Thread navigation arrows smoothly disappear at their respective edges and reappear when scrolling back.
- With classic navigation, the Back button first hides the keyboard and leaves the message form open. A predictive Back gesture retains the single-gesture exit from the form.
- Reworked predictive Back preview retention: ordinary screen locking and backgrounding no longer trigger snapshot clearing.
- Improved the stability of the built-in player during seeking, closing videos and audio playback. Obsolete external WebM extensions are disabled; the built-in player no longer needs them.
- Refined file handling through Share and verification of downloaded extension updates.
- Expanded player, interface and predictive Back diagnostics.

Thanks to everyone for testing, logs and help finding bugs! 🐾
