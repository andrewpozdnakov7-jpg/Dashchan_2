- Added Kohlchan support: browsing boards, threads and attachments, posting, and deleting your own posts. Support is experimental: CAPTCHA and access verification may still be troublesome.
- Improved data persistence and backups. Fixed cases where recent changes or received replies could be lost after the app process ended.
- Improved push reply handling with retries after failures, reply persistence and notification recovery.
- Fixed replaying a finished video in Picture-in-Picture. Also corrected app removal from Recents while PiP is active.
- Improved the image cache, sharing files with other apps, and translation model downloads.
- Popup messages now use the header color by default. This option will also be applied to existing users on the first launch after updating; you can change it again in settings.
- Switched source and target compatibility to Java 17, addressed some warnings and added automated tests. The minimum Android version is unchanged: Android 11.
