- Added “Roll over thread” to the opening post’s long-press menu. It prepares a new thread with the subject, text and attachments, ready to edit and submit. Replacing an existing draft requires confirmation.
- Updated the new video player’s button layout editor. Tap a button to hide or restore it; hiding every button in a panel also hides the panel itself.
- Improved the new posting form: in automatic height mode, attachments no longer collapse the message field. With the keyboard open, attachments remain below and are available by scrolling. The transition from “Done” to “Send” after captcha confirmation is faster.
- Added a “Thread title” toggle to the thread menu: enable it to display the subject in the opening post and copy it.
- Fixed installation of downloaded updates: the app remembers a ready APK and offers to install it when you return. Choosing “Later” schedules another reminder after several new launches.
- Improved repeated PIN and biometric prompts: the selected interval survives Android reclaiming the app process. Restarting the phone still requires authentication again.

New interface: Settings → Experimental features → New Material 3 interface.

App protection is still experimental. Do not rely on it to protect sensitive data; test the additional PIN and file deletion only with expendable data.

Thanks to everyone testing the app, sending logs and helping find bugs! 🐾
